# Sunset Boulevard

Warm and vibrant sunset colors.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Dusk Plum | `#5C2A4C` |
| Secondary | Sunset Coral | `#E86A58` |
| Accent | Golden Amber | `#F5A85F` |
| Surface | Blush | `#FBE6DC` |
| Background | Warm Ivory | `#FFF7F0` |
| Text | Cocoa Noir | `#3B222A` |

## Typography

- **Headers**: Playfair Display — fallback: Georgia, serif
- **Body**: Lato — fallback: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif

## Visual Identity

Coral and amber glowing against a deep plum, like the last hour of golden light on a boulevard. Energetic without shouting; warm without kitsch.

**Best for**: Marketing and brand decks, event announcements, creative pitches, lifestyle content.

## Usage Notes

- Light-mode-first: use `#FFF7F0` as the background with `#3B222A` text. Primary and Secondary carry headers and structure; Surface fills cards, table stripes, and callout boxes.
- Accent is for emphasis only: highlights, links, key data points, and calls to action — never body text or large fills behind text.
- Body text must always be Text on Background (or Background-on-Primary for inverted blocks); Secondary and Accent never carry paragraphs.
- Charts: series order Primary → Secondary → Accent; gridlines in Surface; axis labels in Text.
