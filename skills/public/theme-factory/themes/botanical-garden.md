# Botanical Garden

Fresh and organic garden colors.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Leaf Green | `#4C956C` |
| Secondary | Sprout | `#90BE6D` |
| Accent | Blossom Pink | `#F49CBB` |
| Surface | Dew | `#EAF2E4` |
| Background | Linen White | `#FBFBF4` |
| Text | Garden Soil | `#33402F` |

## Typography

- **Headers**: Lora — fallback: Georgia, serif
- **Body**: Karla — fallback: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif

## Visual Identity

Living greens with a single blossom-pink accent on linen — fresh, optimistic, and organic without drifting into pastel mush.

**Best for**: Health and wellness, community programs, spring campaigns, education content.

## Usage Notes

- Light-mode-first: use `#FBFBF4` as the background with `#33402F` text. Primary and Secondary carry headers and structure; Surface fills cards, table stripes, and callout boxes.
- Accent is for emphasis only: highlights, links, key data points, and calls to action — never body text or large fills behind text.
- Body text must always be Text on Background (or Background-on-Primary for inverted blocks); Secondary and Accent never carry paragraphs.
- Charts: series order Primary → Secondary → Accent; gridlines in Surface; axis labels in Text.
